Privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026.

Who uses this app

Sylva Laptop Sync is a personal tool used only by its author. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it accesses

The app uses the Google Drive drive.file scope. It can read and write only the files and folders it created itself in the author's own Google Drive. It has no access to any other Drive content, to Gmail, to Calendar, or to any other Google service.

What it does with data

The app uploads encrypted bundles from one of the author's computers to a folder in the author's Google Drive, and downloads them on another of the author's computers. The content is encrypted before it leaves the first computer and is decrypted only on the second.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored only on the author's own devices and in the author's own Google Drive. No data is sent to any other server or service. No data is shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party. The app collects no analytics.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data

The author can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the files it created in Google Drive.

Contact

Questions go to the support email listed on the app's Google consent screen.
